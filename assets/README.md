These are drawn inline in `index.html` / `js/scene.js` rather than stored as files, because they animate part by part:

- **Hand and fist:** copied from Throw the Clock's inline SVGs. The fingers are scaled for the clench.
- **Cat:** from Shake Your Fist, extended with puffed-fur, arched-back and hiss poses.
- **Wall and cracks:** from Throw the Clock.
- **New in the same style:** the bed, blanket, nightstand, light bulb and firefly.
